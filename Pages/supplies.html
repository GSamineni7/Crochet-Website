Crochet Supplies

Everything you need to get started!

Crochet Hooks

What is a crochet hook?

A crochet hook is the main tool used to create crochet stitches. It has a small hook at the end that catches and pulls yarn through loops to create your project.

Hook Sizes

Crochet hooks come in different sizes, measured in millimeters. The size of hook you use depends on the yarn you're working with.

Choosing Your First Hook

For beginners, a 4mm or 5mm hook is a great place to start. It is comfortable to hold and works well with many common yarns.

Yarn

What is yarn?

Yarn is the material you use to create crochet projects. It is made by twisting fibers together into a long, continuous strand.

Yarn Materials

Yarn can be made from many different fibers, and each material has its own feel and properties. Some common options include acrylic, cotton, wool, and blends.

Yarn Weights

Yarn comes in different weights, which describe how thick the yarn is. The weight you choose affects the size and appearance of your finished project.

Choosing Your First Yarn

A medium-weight acrylic yarn is a great choice for beginners. It is easy to work with, widely available, and makes your stiches easy to see.

Essentials

The tools you'll want to have before you start crocheting.

Scissors

A small pair of sharp scissors makes cutting yarn quick and easy.

Tapestry Needle

Use a tapestry needle to weave loose yarn ends into your finished project.

Stitch Markers

Stitch markers help you keep track of specific stitches, rounds, or important points in your project.

Nice to Have

These tools can make crocheting a little easier, but you don't need them to get started.

Yarn Holder

A yarn holder keeps your yarn in place and helps prevent it from rolling around while you crochet.

Measuring Tape

A measuring tape can help you check the size of your project and make sure you're following the pattern correctly.

Row Counter

A row counter helps you keep track of how many rows you've crocheted, especially in larger projects.
